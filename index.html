System Architecture

Clinical Decision & Triage Infrastructure

A multi-stage pipeline ensuring deterministic safety rules, machine-learning classification, and evidence-supported knowledge retrieval.

1. Diagnostic & Triage Engine

Natural language understanding to risk classification

  • 1
    Semantic Symptom Extraction

    Sentence Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) identify clinical concepts, duration, and intensity from freeform or spoken descriptions.

  • 2
    Priority Emergency Screen

    Immediate rule-based interruption for cardiac chest pain, focal neurological weakness, respiratory distress, or severe hemorrhage before any model prediction.

  • 3
    Random Forest ML Classification

    Trained clinical classifier assesses symptom vectors against diagnostic databases, generating top possible condition matches with confidence framing.

  • 4
    Independent Severity Assessment

    A separate risk module computes LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH acuity based on reported duration, vulnerable age factors, and progressional indicators.

2. Evidence Retrieval & Care Pathways

Curated medical literature to localized healthcare referrals

  • 1
    FAISS Vector RAG Explanation Layer

    Retrieves verified clinical summaries from curated MedlinePlus databases, explaining why conditions matched and typical symptom duration.

  • 2
    Multi-Turn Conversational Memory

    Persistent SQLite session storage tracks previously reported symptoms, answering follow-up questions intelligently without asking redundant information.

  • 3
    Multilingual Healthcare Bridge

    Accepts queries and provides structured explanations across English, Hindi, Kannada, and Tamil to support non-English speaking rural populations.

  • 4
    Tiered Health Centre Referrals

    Directs patients to appropriate levels of care: Primary Health Centres (PHC), Community Health Centres (CHC), or District Emergency Hospitals.

RuralCare AI Decision & Triage Methodology

RuralCare AI operates a multi-stage clinical decision-support and retrieval pipeline designed to maximize patient safety and explainability.

1
Symptom Presentation

User describes symptoms in plain text or speech across English, Hindi, Kannada, or Tamil.

2
Semantic NLP & Normalization

Sentence Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) map colloquial expressions to standardized clinical symptom entities.

3
Deterministic Emergency Red-Flag Triage

High-priority safety rules screen for stroke signs, acute coronary syndromes, severe dyspnea, and hemorrhage.

4
Random Forest ML Condition Matching

Trained 200-tree clinical classifier computes top possible condition matches with confidence bounds.

5
FAISS Evidence Retrieval (RAG)

Vector database retrieves verified clinical summaries from curated MedlinePlus medical databases.

6
Acuity Assessment & Explanation

Provides clear, transparent explanations of matched indicators, typical illness duration, and when to seek a doctor.

Clinical Scope & Medical Disclaimer

Essential Clinical Notice

RuralCare AI is an assistive decision-support tool created for educational and triage awareness. It does not provide a definitive medical diagnosis and does not prescribe medications.

Clinical Functions Supported:

  • • Assisting patients in structuring and articulating complex symptom descriptions.
  • • Identifying potential condition associations from clinical symptom datasets for educational context.
  • • Prioritizing patient safety through deterministic emergency detection.
  • • Connecting rural communities to verified primary, secondary, and tertiary health facilities.

Exclusions & Prohibitions:

  • • Does NOT make definitive clinical diagnoses.
  • • Does NOT formulate drug dosing or prescription regimens.
  • • Does NOT replace in-person clinical examination or diagnostic laboratory testing.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026 · Institutional Release

1. Scope of Service

RuralCare AI provides automated symptom assessment and healthcare information for educational and pre-clinical triage awareness. By using this service, you acknowledge that RuralCare AI is not a licensed healthcare provider, medical clinic, or emergency dispatch service.

2. Non-Diagnostic Nature

All condition matches, severity ratings, and medical explanations generated by the system represent statistical correlations and educational literature retrieval. They must never be interpreted as confirmed clinical diagnoses. Always consult a registered medical practitioner for medical evaluation.

3. Emergency Situations

If you or someone in your care is experiencing severe chest pain, sudden numbness or paralysis, difficulty breathing, continuous bleeding, seizures, or loss of consciousness, you agree to contact national emergency services (112 in India) or proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency room.

Privacy & Data Ethics Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Public Health Compliance

1. Data Minimization Principle

RuralCare AI operates under strict data minimization. The system does not require you to provide your legal name, national identity number, contact phone number, or residential address to use the symptom checker.

2. Local Browser Storage

Your session identifiers and conversation previews are stored locally in your browser (LocalStorage). You retain the ability to permanently clear all stored local consultation history at any time using the Clear Storage function in Workspace Settings.

3. No Third-Party Commercial Tracking

We do not transmit your health information to third-party advertising exchanges, tracking pixels, or data brokers. Session data is used strictly for maintaining multi-turn context within your active triage session.

4. Geolocation Requests

When searching for nearby hospitals or clinics, geolocation requests are initiated solely on user interaction and handled directly by Google Maps in an external browser tab.
